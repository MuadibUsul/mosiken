AI-Agent Collaboration & Orchestration

Public website · Active product development

Veynod

  1. Tasks
  2. Agent execution
  3. Review
  4. Human decision

Veynod provides a shared environment for teams of AI agents to coordinate work.

It brings tasks, shared project context, agent execution, review steps, and human decisions into one connected workflow. The goal is to make multi-agent development more structured, observable, and manageable without forcing people to supervise every individual step.

  • Multi-Agent Systems
  • Agent Coordination
  • Workflow Orchestration
  • Human Oversight

In development

ANCS

AI Native Creator Studio

  1. Direction in words or voice
  2. Visual and timeline targeting
  3. Non-destructive edit

ANCS explores a more direct way to make and edit video.

Its design combines natural-language direction, responsive voice interaction, precise visual and timeline targeting, non-destructive editing, and multi-model workflows. The objective is to let creators express what they want to change while retaining control over source media and the final result.

  • AI Video Editing
  • Creative Workflows
  • Multimodal Interaction
  • Non-Destructive Editing

AI-Native Social Discovery

Research and product design

Serendipity

  1. Conversational onboarding
  2. Preference modeling
  3. Agent-assisted matching
  4. Your choice

Serendipity is an exploratory social product built around deeper understanding and more intentional real-world connections.

Its concept uses conversational onboarding, individual preference modeling, and AI-agent-assisted matching to help people discover compatible connections while preserving user choice, privacy, and autonomy.

  • Social Discovery
  • Conversational AI
  • Agent-Assisted Matching
  • Human Connection

Engineering & Fabrication Software

Available · Ongoing product evolution

SheetOS

SheetMetal OS

  1. Field measurements
  2. Parametric calculation
  3. Unfolded geometry
  4. DXF output

SheetOS supports sheet-metal engineering workflows, from field measurements and parametric calculations to unfolded geometry and production-ready drawings.

Built for practical fabrication scenarios, the software helps users convert dimensional inputs into usable layouts and DXF output for downstream manufacturing processes. Android and Windows applications are available.

  • Sheet-Metal Design
  • Parametric Geometry
  • Fabrication
  • DXF Export

About

Independent thinking. Purposeful engineering.

Mosiken LLC develops original software products from concept to commercial release. We work across multiple disciplines because important problems rarely fit into a single category. What connects our products is a focus on thoughtful user experiences, robust software foundations, and the practical value of new technology.

Company

Mosiken LLC is an independent software company organized in the State of Wyoming, United States, founded and wholly owned by Chen Su. We research, design, and develop proprietary software products across AI-agent collaboration, intelligent creative workflows, mobile social discovery, and specialized engineering CAD tools.

Our focus is on building and operating original first-party software rather than reselling or white-labeling third-party tools. We design systems around real workflows and ship software across cloud SaaS, desktop (Windows and macOS), and mobile platforms (iOS and Android).

Mosiken LLC serves individual professionals and teams in the United States and internationally through digital software licenses, SaaS subscriptions, and global app marketplaces including the Apple App Store and Google Play.

Mission

Vision

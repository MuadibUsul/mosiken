Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026. This policy explains how Mosiken LLC collects, uses, and protects information across our websites and software products.

Controller & Scope

Mosiken LLC (Wyoming Filing ID: 2026-002137028; Registered Office: 30 N Gould St Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA) operates mosiken.org, our dedicated product domains (including veynod.com and sushiheng.com), and our desktop, web, and mobile applications (Veynod, ANCS, Serendipity, and SheetOS).

For privacy inquiries or data requests, contact us at contact@mosiken.org.

Information We Collect

Account & Contact Data: When you create an account, purchase a license or subscription, or write to us, we collect your name, email address, account settings, and correspondence.

Billing Metadata: Purchases and subscriptions are processed by PCI-DSS compliant providers such as Stripe, the Apple App Store, and Google Play. Mosiken LLC receives payment confirmation and plan status, and never stores full payment card numbers on our servers.

Product Inputs & Diagnostics: We process project parameters, CAD dimensions, and workflow inputs solely to deliver requested software features, alongside minimal diagnostic and security logs.

Use & Data Protection

We use information solely to operate, maintain, secure, and improve our software, fulfill subscriptions and support requests, and send transactional notices.

No Sale of Personal Data: Mosiken LLC does not sell or rent personal data to third parties or data brokers. Private project files and engineering drawings are never used to train public models without explicit opt-in consent.

Retention & Your Rights

We retain personal information only as long as required to provide active services or meet U.S. tax and legal obligations, protected with TLS encryption in transit and strict access controls.

You may request access to, correction of, export of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing contact@mosiken.org.

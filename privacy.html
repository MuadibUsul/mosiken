Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 18, 2026 · Last Updated: November 24, 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how Mosiken LLC collects, uses, and safeguards information across our websites and software products.

1. Data Controller & Scope

Mosiken LLC (“Mosiken,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), a limited liability company organized under the laws of the State of Wyoming, United States (Filing ID: 2026-002137028; Registered Office: 30 N Gould St Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA), is the operator and data controller for mosiken.org, our dedicated product domains including veynod.com and sushiheng.com, and our proprietary desktop, web, and mobile software applications (including Veynod, ANCS, Serendipity, and SheetOS, collectively the “Services”).

For privacy inquiries or data subject requests, you may contact our managing member directly at contact@mosiken.org.

2. Information We Collect

Account & Contact Information: When you register for an account, purchase a license or subscription, or contact us by email, we collect your name, email address, account preferences, and correspondence records.

Billing & Transaction Metadata: Paid subscriptions, software licenses, and in-app purchases are processed by PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment platforms, such as Stripe, Apple App Store, and Google Play. Mosiken LLC receives confirmation of payment status, plan tier, and billing country, and never collects or stores full credit card numbers or bank credentials on our servers.

Workspace, CAD & Project Content: Depending on the product you use, we process user-supplied inputs such as parametric geometry dimensions in SheetOS (many calculations run locally on your device), task orchestration data in Veynod, or user-submitted media and prompts in AI-enabled features solely to provide the requested functionality.

Technical & Diagnostic Data: We collect minimal server logs and crash diagnostics (such as browser or OS version, IP address for security rate-limiting, and error traces) necessary to keep the Services reliable and secure.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information strictly to: (a) deliver, maintain, authenticate, and improve our software products; (b) fulfill software licenses, subscriptions, and customer support requests; (c) send essential transactional notices such as receipts, security alerts, and policy updates; and (d) detect fraud, abuse, or unauthorized access.

No Sale of Personal Data: Mosiken LLC does not sell, rent, or broker your personal information to data brokers or third-party advertisers. Private project files and engineering drawings are not used to train public foundation models without your explicit opt-in consent.

4. Infrastructure & Third-Party Processors

We rely on reputable infrastructure and service providers (including Cloudflare for network delivery and security, cloud hosting providers in the United States, and payment processors such as Stripe, Apple, and Google) bound by confidentiality and data-processing obligations. Information may be processed and stored in the United States and other jurisdictions where our infrastructure providers operate.

5. Data Retention & Security

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to provide active Services, comply with United States tax and accounting obligations, or resolve disputes. We apply industry-standard safeguards including TLS encryption in transit, access controls, and least-privilege credential management.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including U.S. state privacy laws, the GDPR/UK GDPR, and other applicable frameworks), you have the right to request access to, correction of, export of, or deletion of your personal information. To exercise these rights, email contact@mosiken.org from the address associated with your account. We respond to verified requests within 30 days.

7. Children’s Privacy & Policy Updates

Our Services are intended for general professional and adult audiences and are not directed to children under 13 (or 16 where applicable). Material updates to this Privacy Policy will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
